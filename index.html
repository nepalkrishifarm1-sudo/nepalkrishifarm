Our Story

Rooted in Jhapa,
Growing for Nepal

Nepal Krishi Tatha Pasupanchi Farm started with a simple idea — that Nepal's fertile land can grow world-class organic pineapples while creating real work for local families. From our base in Bhadrapur, Jhapa, we rely only on organic compost, natural fertilizers, and farming methods that look after the land instead of wearing it down.

We believe in keeping talent in Nepal. Our farm creates stable jobs for local youth and farmers.

The Man Behind the Vision

Mr. Manoj Singh
Founder · Leading Agricultural Entrepreneur & Pineapple Pioneer, Jhapa, Nepal

Manoj Singh is a well-known agricultural entrepreneur from Jyamirgadi-1, Jhapa, and one of Nepal's most successful large-scale pineapple farmers. Born in 2036 BS into a large family that struggled financially, he worked his way through early hardship with sheer persistence. What began as a small plot has grown into a multi-million rupee farming business — and helped push a whole generation in eastern Nepal to take modern agriculture seriously.

Humble Beginnings

Raised alongside seven siblings, Manoj completed his education up to the secondary level under difficult financial conditions.

The Turning Point (2060/61 BS)

His commercial farming journey officially began with just 7,000 pineapple saplings gifted to him by his father-in-law.

Scale of Operations

Today, his core pineapple plantation alone holds over 500,000+ plants spanning 28–30 bighas of land.

Diversification

Beyond pineapples, Manoj has scaled into tea plantation management, leasing roughly 90 bighas in Bhadrapur and employing 100–150 local workers daily.

Impact & Philosophy

With a net annual profit of 20–22 lakh NPR from pineapples alone and an overall enterprise now worth crores, Manoj is living proof that dedicated commercial farming pays off in Nepal. Even with all that success, he's stayed close to his roots.

"If I had chosen any other profession, achieving my current status would have been impossible. I am fully content, happy, and see my agricultural profession as my true fortune."
— Manoj Singh

His son, Raju Singh, now carries this legacy forward — modernizing the farm's operations for a new generation.

Mr. Raju Singh
Leadership
Mr. Raju Singh
Managing Director · Nepal Krishi Tatha Pasupanchi Farm

A young and passionate entrepreneur, Raju Singh runs Nepal Krishi Tatha Pasupanchi Farm on a simple belief: Nepal's youth shouldn't have to leave the country to find opportunity. As the son of founder Manoj Singh, Raju is carrying the family legacy forward, bringing fresh ideas and technology into the day-to-day work of the farm. With deep roots in Jhapa and a clear vision for Nepal's agricultural future, he's built a model farm that grows premium organic pineapples while proving that world-class work can happen right here at home.

"I started this farm to prove one thing: Nepal has everything it takes. Our land is fertile, our people are skilled, and our pineapples are second to none. Why go abroad when our country needs us to build it here?"
— Raju Singh
